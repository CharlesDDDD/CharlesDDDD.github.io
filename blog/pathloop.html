Pathloop: Making a Looped MoE Take Different PathsPathloop：让循环 MoE 走出不同的路径

A looped mixture-of-experts runs the same layers three times. Does it do anything different on each pass?一个循环版的 MoE 会把同样的层跑三遍。每一遍到底有没有做不一样的事？

Chunyuan Deng · August–September 2026 · project logChunyuan Deng · 2026 年 8–9 月 · 项目日志

TL;DR

We fine-tune a 30B mixture-of-experts model with its middle layers looped three times, which turns 48 layers into 96 executed ones. Looping helps, but not where you would guess: on the coding data we train on it adds nothing, and on the nine benchmarks we never train on it does not raise the peak either — it holds the peak. Plain fine-tuning gains five points out-of-domain and then gives all of them back; the looped runs keep most of theirs. Reading the probes then showed why the loop is weaker than it should be — the second and third passes are near-copies of each other. Pathloop was our attempt to force the passes apart by making the router sample a little during training and then letting the passes compete.我们把一个 30B 的 MoE 模型的中间层循环三次再做微调，48 层因此变成执行 96 层。循环确实有用，但用处不在你以为的地方：在我们训练所用的代码数据上它没有任何增益，在九个从未训练过的 benchmark 上它也没有把峰值抬高——它做的是守住峰值。普通微调在域外先涨五个点，然后又全部吐回去；循环版则保住了大部分。之后读探针数据才明白循环为什么比预想的弱——第二遍和第三遍几乎是彼此的复制品。Pathloop 就是我们试图把这几遍掰开的办法：训练时让 router 做一点采样，再让各遍互相竞争。

Pathloop has now run, and the result is a negative one worth writing down. It buys about a point of coding pass@1 over plain fine-tuning — small, but reproducible and outside a bootstrap CI. It does not beat plain looped SFT by anything we can resolve. More importantly, the reward signal the whole method rests on was being measured with a broken instrument: the logit lens we scored each pass with recovered 0.8 of the 11.7 nats the hidden state actually carries, so it was reading noise at 7% of scale, and its apparent ordering of the passes was an artifact of depth, not quality. Replacing the lens with a full readout through the remaining layers fixed the instrument and delivered the real answer: the three passes are equally good, to within 3%. There was never anything to select between. A direct token-level probe says why — pass 2 changes 0.4 of a token's 8 experts relative to pass 1, against 2.6 for pass 1 relative to pass 0. And on the full 14-benchmark suite the arms sort not by pathloop tier but by where the gate was initialised: all four arms whose gate starts at zero peak at the earliest checkpoint and decline, and all three whose gate starts above zero are at their worst there and climb. The binding constraint is the gate, not the router, and that is what we are testing next.Pathloop 现在跑完了，结果是一个值得写下来的负结果。相对普通微调，它买来约一个点的代码 pass@1——很小，但可复现，且 bootstrap 置信区间不含零。相对普通的循环 SFT，它的优势小到我们分辨不出来。更重要的是，整个方法赖以成立的奖励信号，是用一件坏掉的仪器量出来的：我们给每一遍打分用的 logit lens，只从隐状态里取出了 11.7 nats 中的 0.8 nats，也就是在 7% 的量程上读噪声；它给出的那个「各遍排序」，反映的是深度，不是质量。把 lens 换成「继续跑完剩余层再读出」之后，仪器修好了，真正的答案也出来了：三遍一样好，差距在 3% 以内。从一开始就没有什么可挑的。一个直接的 token 级探针解释了原因——相对第 1 遍，第 2 遍只改变了一个 token 8 个 expert 中的 0.4 个，而第 1 遍相对第 0 遍改变了 2.6 个。而在完整的 14 个 benchmark 上，各组的排序不是按 pathloop 的 tier 分的，而是按门控的初始值：四个门控从 0 开始的组，都在最早的 checkpoint 达到峰值然后下滑；三个门控从非零开始的组，都在那里最差然后往上爬。真正的约束是门控，不是 router，这也是我们接下来要测的。

What does fine-tuning actually train in a mixture-of-experts model?微调在 MoE 模型里到底训练了什么？

Start with a single MoE layer. A token arrives with hidden state $h_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$, and a small linear router scores every one of the $E$ experts:先看单个 MoE 层。一个 token 带着隐状态 $h_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$ 进来，一个小的线性 router 给全部 $E$ 个 expert 打分：

$$s_t \;=\; \mathrm{softmax}\big(W_r\, h_t\big) \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{E}.$$

In the model we use, $E = 128$. Evaluating all of them would defeat the purpose, so the layer keeps only the best $k = 8$,我们用的模型里 $E = 128$。把它们全算一遍就失去了意义，所以这一层只保留得分最高的 $k = 8$ 个，

$$\mathcal{S}_t \;=\; \operatorname*{arg\,top-}_{k}\; s_t,$$

renormalises the scores over the survivors, and mixes their outputs:在留下来的这些 expert 上重新归一化分数，再混合它们的输出：

$$\mathrm{MoE}(h_t) \;=\; \sum_{e \in \mathcal{S}_t} \frac{s_{t,e}}{\sum_{e' \in \mathcal{S}_t} s_{t,e'}}\; f_e(h_t), \qquad f_e(h) = W^e_{\text{down}}\big(\sigma(W^e_{\text{gate}}h) \odot W^e_{\text{up}}h\big).$$

We will call $\mathcal{S}_t$ the token's path: the eight-expert route it takes through this layer. Stack 48 such layers and a token's full journey through the model is a sequence of 48 such choices.我们把 $\mathcal{S}_t$ 叫做这个 token 的路径：它穿过这一层时走的那条八专家路线。叠 48 层，一个 token 在模型里的完整旅程就是 48 次这样的选择。

Now the part that matters for fine-tuning. The other $E-k = 120$ experts are never evaluated, so they appear nowhere in the computation graph, so nothing flows back to them:接下来是对微调最关键的一点。另外 $E-k = 120$ 个 expert 从未被计算，因此不出现在计算图里，也就没有任何梯度回流到它们身上：

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \theta_e} \;=\; 0 \qquad \text{for every } e \notin \mathcal{S}_t.$$

Fine-tuning therefore trains the experts that the pretrained router already liked. In principle the router could learn to like different ones — $W_r$ does receive gradient. In practice it barely moves: over a full epoch we measure the routing entropy shifting by 2% and top-1 confidence by 4% (see the probes). Whatever paths pretraining laid down, fine-tuning walks along them.所以微调训练的，是预训练阶段 router 本来就偏爱的那些 expert。原则上 router 可以学着换一批——$W_r$ 确实拿得到梯度。但实际上它几乎不动：跑完整整一个 epoch，我们测到路由熵变化 2%，top-1 置信度变化 4%（见探针那节）。预训练铺下什么路，微调就沿着什么路走。

So here is the question this project starts from. Can we give a token more than one path through the same weights?于是这个项目的出发点就是一个问题：能不能让一个 token 在同一套权重里走出不止一条路？

Looping the middle layers把中间层循环起来

The simplest way to get a second path is to run the same layers a second time. Write one transformer layer as拿到第二条路径最简单的办法，就是把同样的层再跑一遍。把一层 transformer 写成

$$T_\ell(h) \;=\; z + \mathrm{MoE}_\ell\big(\mathrm{Norm}(z)\big), \qquad z \;=\; h + \mathrm{Attn}_\ell\big(\mathrm{Norm}(h)\big),$$

and pick a window of consecutive layers to repeat — for us layers $[a,b) = [12,36)$, so $W = 24$ layers wide, sitting in the middle of the 48. Call the composition of that window $F = T_{b-1} \circ \cdots \circ T_a$.再挑一段连续的层作为重复窗口——我们取 $[a,b) = [12,36)$，也就是 $W = 24$ 层宽，位于 48 层的中间。把这个窗口的复合记作 $F = T_{b-1} \circ \cdots \circ T_a$。

A token first runs the layers below the window, once:token 先把窗口下方的层跑一遍：

$$h_{-1} \;=\; \big(T_{a-1} \circ \cdots \circ T_0\big)\big(\mathrm{Embed}(x)\big).$$

Then the window runs $K = 3$ times. Each pass $\tau$ produces a candidate state然后窗口跑 $K = 3$ 遍。每一遍 $\tau$ 产生一个候选状态

$$u_\tau \;=\; F\big(h_{\tau-1}\big), \qquad \tau = 0, 1, \dots, K-1,$$

and the obvious question is how to combine the passes. Simply chaining them ($h_\tau = u_\tau$) makes the model three times deeper on day one, which is a large and untested change to a pretrained network. Instead each pass after the first gets a learned gate $g_\tau \in \mathbb{R}^d$ that decides, per channel, how much of the new candidate to accept:接下来的问题自然是如何把各遍合并。直接串起来（$h_\tau = u_\tau$）等于第一天就把模型变深三倍，对一个预训练网络来说是一次很大且未经验证的改动。所以从第二遍起，每一遍都配一个可学习的门控 $g_\tau \in \mathbb{R}^d$，逐通道决定接受多少新候选：

$$h_0 \;=\; u_0, \qquad h_\tau \;=\; h_{\tau-1} \;+\; g_\tau \odot \big(u_\tau - h_{\tau-1}\big) \quad (\tau \geq 1).$$

Finally the layers above the window run once on the last state:最后，窗口上方的层在最终状态上跑一遍：

$$\text{logits} \;=\; W_U\, \mathrm{Norm}\Big(\big(T_{L-1} \circ \cdots \circ T_b\big)\big(h_{K-1}\big)\Big).$$

The token has now been through $a + KW + (L-b) = 12 + 72 + 12 = 96$ executed layers using 48 layers' worth of weights. The only new parameters anywhere are the $2d$ gate values.至此这个 token 一共经过了 $a + KW + (L-b) = 12 + 72 + 12 = 96$ 层的执行，而用的只是 48 层的权重。整套改动里新增的参数只有 $2d$ 个门控值。

Why do the gates start at zero?门控为什么要从 0 开始？

Set $g_\tau = 0$ and the update above gives $h_\tau = h_{\tau-1}$ for every $\tau \geq 1$, so $h_{K-1} = u_0 = F(h_{-1})$ and the looped model reproduces the base model bit for bit. Fine-tuning then starts from a known-good point and opens the loop only as far as the loss rewards. That is a real advantage: there is no initialisation shock, and no risk that a promising idea is buried under the damage of turning a 48-layer model into a 96-layer one overnight.令 $g_\tau = 0$，上面的更新式对所有 $\tau \geq 1$ 都给出 $h_\tau = h_{\tau-1}$，于是 $h_{K-1} = u_0 = F(h_{-1})$，循环模型逐比特地复现基座模型。微调因此是从一个已知良好的点出发，损失奖励多少，就把循环打开多少。这是实实在在的好处：没有初始化冲击，也不会出现「一个有前途的想法，被一夜之间把 48 层模型变成 96 层的破坏所掩盖」的情况。

It also has a cost, and the cost turns out to dominate everything else in this post. Because $W_r$ is shared across passes, pass $\tau$ can route differently from pass $\tau-1$ only if the router's input moved enough to reorder its top eight. From the update rule, that movement is它也有代价，而且这个代价最后主导了本文的其他一切。由于 $W_r$ 在各遍之间共享，第 $\tau$ 遍要想路由得和第 $\tau-1$ 遍不同，router 的输入就必须移动到足以改变 top-8 的排序。按更新式，这个移动量是

$$h_\tau - h_{\tau-1} \;=\; g_\tau \odot \big(u_\tau - h_{\tau-1}\big),$$

which is proportional to the gate. So small gates give small movement, small movement gives the same routing:它正比于门控。所以门控小 → 移动小，移动小 → 路由不变：

$$\|g_\tau\| \to 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_\tau \to h_{\tau-1} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal{S}^{\tau}_t = \mathcal{S}^{\tau-1}_t.$$

The safe initialisation and the goal are in tension: the gates start where the passes are guaranteed identical, and they have to travel a long way before the passes are meaningfully different. We measure how far they actually travel below — the answer is $\|g_\tau\|_{\text{rms}} \approx 0.02$ after a full epoch, and the passes are indeed near-copies.安全的初始化和我们的目标是冲突的：门控起步的位置恰好保证各遍完全相同，而要让各遍出现有意义的差别，它得走很远。下面会测它实际走了多远——答案是跑完一个 epoch 后 $\|g_\tau\|_{\text{rms}} \approx 0.02$，各遍确实是近似的复制品。

Pathloop: forcing the passes apartPathloop：把各遍强行掰开

The probes below say this tension resolves the wrong way: passes 1 and 2 are near-copies of each other, and a direct token-level measurement puts the difference at four tenths of one expert out of eight. Pathloop attacks that from the router's side rather than the gate's. If the passes will not become different on their own, make them different by construction: during training, let each pass sample part of its own path. Inference is untouched — the served model is plain top-$k$ routing.下面的探针说明，这个张力是朝错误的一侧收敛的：第 1 遍和第 2 遍几乎是彼此的复制，而直接的 token 级测量把差别定在八个 expert 里的 0.4 个。Pathloop 选择从 router 这一侧、而不是门控那一侧下手。既然各遍自己分不开，那就在构造上让它们不同：训练时，让每一遍自己采样出一部分路径。推理不受影响——上线的模型仍是普通的 top-$k$ 路由。

What if the router sampled part of the path?如果让 router 采样一部分路径呢？

Split the budget $k = p + q$. The first $p$ experts are the model's own top choices, kept as-is:把预算拆成 $k = p + q$。前 $p$ 个 expert 是模型自己的最优选择，原样保留：

$$\mathcal{P}_t \;=\; \operatorname*{arg\,top-}_{p}\big\{\, s_{t,e} \;:\; e \in \mathcal{S}_t \,\big\}.$$

The remaining $q$ slots are for exploration. If every pass sampled from the same pool of non-selected experts they would frequently draw the same ones, which is exactly the collision we are trying to avoid. So we first partition the experts by a random assignment $\beta$, redrawn every step, which hands each pass a disjoint candidate pool:剩下的 $q$ 个名额留给探索。如果每一遍都从同一批未被选中的 expert 里采样，它们会经常抽到同样的几个，而这正是我们想避免的碰撞。所以先用一个每步重新抽取的随机分配 $\beta$ 把 expert 划分开，让每一遍拿到互不相交的候选池：

$$\beta : \{1,\dots,E\} \to \{0,\dots,K\!-\!1\}, \qquad \mathcal{T}^\tau_t \;=\; \big\{\, e \notin \mathcal{S}_t \;:\; \beta(e) = \tau \,\big\}.$$

Within its own pool, pass $\tau$ draws $q$ experts without replacement, with probability proportional to the router's own scores. The standard trick for this is Gumbel top-$q$:在自己的池子里，第 $\tau$ 遍按 router 自身分数成正比的概率、不放回地抽 $q$ 个 expert。标准做法是 Gumbel top-$q$：

$$\mathcal{Q}^\tau_t \;=\; \operatorname*{arg\,top-}_{q}\Big\{\, \log s_{t,e} - \log(-\log u_e) \;:\; e \in \mathcal{T}^\tau_t \,\Big\}, \qquad u_e \sim \mathcal{U}(0,1).$$

The path this pass actually takes is the union, renormalised over its own members:这一遍实际走的路径就是两者的并集，并在自己的成员上重新归一化：

$$\tilde{\mathcal{S}}^\tau_t \;=\; \mathcal{P}_t \cup \mathcal{Q}^\tau_t, \qquad \big|\tilde{\mathcal{S}}^\tau_t\big| = p + q = k.$$

Two things are worth noticing. The cost is unchanged — still exactly $k$ experts evaluated per token, so this is not a compute-for-quality trade. And the $q$ sampled experts now sit in the computation graph, so they receive gradient, which the zero-gradient property above otherwise forbids. With $q = 0$ the whole construction collapses back to ordinary top-$k$, which is what makes it safe to schedule.有两点值得注意。开销没有变——每个 token 仍然只算 $k$ 个 expert，所以这不是「用算力换质量」的交易。另外，被采样的 $q$ 个 expert 现在进入了计算图，因此能拿到梯度，而前面那条零梯度性质本来是禁止这件事的。当 $q = 0$ 时，整套构造退化回普通 top-$k$，这正是它可以安全地用调度控制的原因。

Which pass was better?哪一遍更好？

Sampling only helps if we can tell a good draw from a bad one. Because every pass produces a full-width hidden state, we can read each one through the model's own output head and score it on the true next token:只有能分辨出好抽样和坏抽样，采样才有意义。由于每一遍都产生一个全宽的隐状态，我们可以让它过一遍模型自己的输出头，再用真实的下一个 token 给它打分：

$$R_\tau(t) \;=\; \log P\big(y_t \;\big|\; W_U\,\mathrm{Norm}(h_\tau)_t\big).$$

This is a logit lens: a cheap measure of how much of the answer pass $\tau$ has already assembled. It is not calibrated in absolute terms — mid-depth states read poorly through a head trained on final-depth states — but we only ever use differences.这就是一个 logit lens：一种便宜的度量，衡量第 $\tau$ 遍已经把答案组装到了什么程度。它在绝对意义上没有校准——中间深度的状态，用一个按最终深度训练的输出头去读效果很差——但我们只用它们之间的差。

This paragraph is wrong, and it is the central mistake of the project. The argument that miscalibration is harmless because only differences are used does not survive contact with the numbers: the lens recovers 0.79 of the 11.71 nats the state actually carries, and it ranks the passes by how far they have drifted from the head's expectations rather than by quality. The measurement section works through it and gives the fix. The construction below is stated as we built it.上面这段是错的，而且它是整个项目最核心的错误。「因为只用差值，所以校准不准没关系」这个论证，一碰到具体数字就站不住了：这个 lens 只从状态实际携带的 11.71 nats 中取出了 0.79 nats，而且它给各遍排序的依据，是它们偏离输出头预期的程度，而不是质量。测量那一节会把它讲清楚并给出修正。下面的构造，仍按我们当初搭建的样子陈述。

Which is the second point. The $K$ passes saw the same token in the same context, so comparing a pass against its own siblings cancels token difficulty exactly, with no baseline model and no value network:这就带出第二点。$K$ 遍看到的是同一个上下文里的同一个 token，所以拿一遍跟它自己的兄弟比较，可以精确抵消 token 的难度，既不需要 baseline 模型，也不需要 value 网络：

$$A_\tau(t) \;=\; R_\tau(t) \;-\; \frac{1}{K-1}\sum_{\tau' \neq \tau} R_{\tau'}(t), \qquad \sum_\tau A_\tau(t) = 0.$$

The two training terms两个训练项

Given an advantage per pass, the sampled experts are reinforced by an ordinary policy gradient, where $\pi_\tau$ is the router's probability of having drawn that particular set as an ordered draw without replacement:有了每一遍的优势值，被采样的 expert 就可以用普通的策略梯度来强化，其中 $\pi_\tau$ 是 router 以不放回有序抽样方式抽到这一组的概率：

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{path}} \;=\; -\sum_{\tau} \mathbb{E}_t\Big[\, A_\tau(t)\; \log \pi_\tau\big(\mathcal{Q}^\tau_t \mid h_{\tau-1}\big) \Big].$$

A second term pushes the passes to improve monotonically, weighting later passes more — on an assumption about the passes that later turned out to be false:第二项推动各遍单调改进，给靠后的遍更大权重——它所依赖的那个关于各遍的假设，后来被证明是错的：

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ladder}} \;=\; -\sum_{\tau} \frac{\lambda\,(\tau+1)}{K}\; \mathbb{E}_t\big[\, R_\tau(t) \,\big].$$

Why score each pass against the label rather than, say, rewarding the passes for agreeing with each other? Because the cheapest way to make passes agree is to send $g_\tau \to 0$ — the degenerate solution the gates are already sitting next to. Scoring against the label makes the shortcut worthless.为什么要拿标签给每一遍打分，而不是奖励各遍彼此一致？因为让各遍一致最省事的办法就是把 $g_\tau$ 推向 0——而门控本来就紧挨着这个退化解。用标签打分，这条捷径就一文不值了。

Both terms are multiplied by the exploration schedule and vanish wherever $q = 0$, so $q = 0$ is exactly plain looped SFT, bit for bit. We run this in two stages. Tier 1 samples and logs $R_\tau$ and $A_\tau$ without training on them: it asks whether the comparison carries any signal at all before anything is built on top of it. Tier 2 switches on the two losses. In both, $q$ follows a schedule that returns to $0$ before training ends, so the final model is trained under the same policy it is served with.两项都乘上探索调度系数，在 $q = 0$ 处消失，所以 $q = 0$ 就是逐比特等价的普通循环 SFT。我们分两级来跑。Tier 1 只采样并记录 $R_\tau$ 和 $A_\tau$，不拿它们训练：先问清楚这个比较到底有没有信号，再往上搭东西。Tier 2 打开这两个损失。两级里 $q$ 都按调度在训练结束前回到 $0$，这样最终模型训练时的策略与上线时一致。

Why does this happen, and why does it matter?为什么会这样，它为什么重要？

Pass 0 is ungated and always applied in full, so it does real work. Passes 1 and 2 are multiplied by a gate that reaches only about 0.02–0.06, so they nudge the state rather than transform it. A nudged state produces nearly the same router scores, so it selects nearly the same experts — and the two nudges land in nearly the same place as each other.第 0 遍没有门控、永远全量生效，所以它干的是真活。第 1、2 遍要乘上一个只到 0.02–0.06 的门控，因此它们只是轻推状态，而不是改造状态。被轻推过的状态给出的 router 分数几乎不变，于是选中的 expert 也几乎不变——而两次轻推最后落在几乎同一个地方。

This is a problem for pathloop specifically. Its whole mechanism is to compare passes against each other and reward the better one. If the passes are copies, every comparison is between identical things, the advantage $A_\tau$ is zero, and there is nothing to learn from. We confirmed this directly: in short test runs the spread of reward across passes sat at roughly $10^{-7}$, i.e. exactly zero.这对 pathloop 来说是致命的。它的整套机制就是把各遍互相比较、奖励更好的那一遍。如果各遍是复制品，每次比较都是在比两个完全一样的东西，优势值 $A_\tau$ 为零，也就没有任何可学的东西。我们直接验证过：在短测试运行里，各遍之间奖励的离散度大约是 $10^{-7}$，也就是精确的零。

It is also a caveat on the claim of "96 effective layers". By this measurement the model behaves closer to 48 real layers plus two cheap refinement passes.这也是对「96 层有效深度」这个说法的一条保留意见。按这个测量，模型的行为更接近 48 层真实层，外加两遍廉价的微调式精修。

The reward signal was read through a broken instrument奖励信号是用一件坏掉的仪器读出来的

Pathloop scores each pass by pushing its hidden state through the model's own output head — the logit lens of the method section, $R_\tau = \log P(y_t \mid W_U \mathrm{Norm}(h_\tau))$. The state being read sits at layer 36 of 48. The head was trained on layer-48 states. We knew the readout would be poorly calibrated in absolute terms and argued that this was fine because only differences are used.Pathloop 给每一遍打分的方式，是把它的隐状态推过模型自己的输出头——也就是方法一节里的 logit lens，$R_\tau = \log P(y_t \mid W_U \mathrm{Norm}(h_\tau))$。被读取的状态位于 48 层中的第 36 层，而输出头是在第 48 层的状态上训练的。我们知道这个读出在绝对意义上校准很差，但当时认为没关系，因为只用差值。

With a working instrument, the passes are equally good换上能用的仪器，各遍一样好

The fix is not subtle: instead of reading the pass-$\tau$ state through the head directly, run it through the layers that would normally follow — the twelve above the loop window — and then through the head. We call it the coda. It is the honest question the lens was a cheap proxy for: if we stopped looping now and finished the forward pass, how good would the answer be?修法一点也不微妙：不要把第 $\tau$ 遍的状态直接送进输出头，而是先让它跑完本来在后面的那些层——循环窗口上方的十二层——再进输出头。我们把它叫做 coda。它才是 lens 那个廉价代理真正想问的诚实问题：如果现在就停止循环、把前向跑完，答案会有多好？

$$R^{\mathrm{coda}}_\tau(t) \;=\; \log P\Big(y_t \;\Big|\; W_U\,\mathrm{Norm}\big((T_{L-1} \circ \cdots \circ T_b)(h_\tau)\big)_t\Big).$$

It costs $K-1$ extra partial forward passes, which is affordable because the readouts for all $K$ passes are concatenated into a single matrix multiply against the head, and because the states can be detached when only the metric is wanted. The generalisable part可以推广的部分

Give every diagnostic an absolute reference before you trust a difference. Our lens had a relative check (beat shuffled labels) and passed it comfortably while operating at 7% of scale. The absolute reference — $\ln V$ for a next-token readout — costs one line and would have failed it at step 0 of the first run. Relative controls tell you a number is not nothing. They cannot tell you it is not noise.在相信任何差值之前，先给每个诊断量一个绝对参照。我们的 lens 有一个相对检查（赢过打乱标签），它在只发挥 7% 量程的情况下轻松通过。而绝对参照——下一 token 读出的 $\ln V$——只要一行代码，在第一次运行的第 0 步就会让它不及格。相对对照只能告诉你这个数不是什么都没有，不能告诉你它不是噪声。

A control arm only controls for what it shares with the treatment. An unlooped baseline cannot detect a bug in the looped serving path. When the treatment adds a code path, the control has to exercise it.对照组只能控制它与实验组共有的那部分。一个不带循环的基线，检测不出循环部署路径上的 bug。当实验组新增了一条代码路径，对照组就必须也走一遍这条路径。

Measure the mechanism before optimising the objective. Every bug in the list above delayed pathloop. None of them mattered, because the expert-set overlap probe — four lines, no gradient, one boolean AND per layer — would have shown before any of the losses were written that pass 2 changes 0.4 of 8 experts relative to pass 1, and therefore that there was nothing for a comparison across passes to compare.先测机制，再优化目标。上面列出的每个 bug 都拖慢了 pathloop。但它们其实都不重要，因为那个 expert 集合重叠度探针——四行代码、不需要梯度、每层一次布尔与——在任何一个损失被写出来之前就能显示：相对第 1 遍，第 2 遍只改变 8 个 expert 里的 0.4 个，因此跨遍比较根本没有东西可比。

What we would do differently, and what is running now如果重来会怎么做，以及现在在跑什么

Pathloop attacked the router. The evidence says the router was never the binding constraint: it re-routes 33% of a token's experts on the first extra pass entirely on its own, without any help from sampling, and forcing half the expert budget to be drawn stochastically does not move where it wants to go. What it does not do is re-route again on the second extra pass, and that is a property of the state it is being handed, not of the router reading it.Pathloop 攻击的是 router。而证据表明 router 从来就不是那个卡住的约束：在第一次额外遍上，它完全靠自己就重新路由了一个 token 33% 的 expert，不需要任何采样帮忙；把一半的 expert 预算强行交给随机采样，也不改变它想去的地方。它没做到的，是在第二次额外遍上再重新路由一次——而这是它拿到的那个状态的性质，不是读这个状态的 router 的性质。

Which points at the gate. The earlier version of this post ended by guessing the same thing, and it is one of the few guesses here that survived contact with the data:线索指向门控。本文的早先版本在结尾处猜的也是这件事，而它是本文为数不多、经受住数据检验的猜测之一：

“...then the passes are copies no matter what the router does, and the honest next step is not more pathloop but changing how the loop is built — starting the gates away from zero, so the passes are distinct from the beginning.”「……那么不论 router 怎么做，各遍都是复制品。诚实的下一步就不是再加 pathloop，而是改变循环的搭法——让门控从非零处起步，使各遍从一开始就不同。」

That is the run in progress. It sets $g_\tau = 1$ at initialisation, which by the update rule $h_\tau = h_{\tau-1} + g_\tau \odot (u_\tau - h_{\tau-1})$ gives $h_\tau = u_\tau = F(h_{\tau-1})$ — the plain unweighted loop, three genuine applications of the window, no interpolation with the previous state. Everything else is held at the looped-SFT arm's settings, so the initialisation is the only variable against a known 68.88. Pathloop is present but inert: sampling off, no loss attached, the coda readout still logging $\mathrm{NLL}$ per pass. It is there as an instrument, which is what it should probably have been from the start.这就是正在跑的那次运行。它把 $g_\tau$ 初始化为 1，按更新式 $h_\tau = h_{\tau-1} + g_\tau \odot (u_\tau - h_{\tau-1})$ 得到 $h_\tau = u_\tau = F(h_{\tau-1})$——也就是不加权的朴素循环，窗口被真真切切地施加三次，不与前一状态做插值。其余一切都保持循环 SFT 分支的设置，所以初始化是相对已知的 68.88 的唯一变量。Pathloop 在场但处于惰性状态：采样关闭、不挂损失，coda 读出仍逐遍记录 $\mathrm{NLL}$。它现在只是一件仪器——这大概本来就该是它从一开始的角色。

This gives up the property that made the gated loop attractive — at $g=1$ the model no longer reproduces the base checkpoint on step 0, and the first steps are genuinely a 96-layer network that has never been trained as one. That is the trade the evidence now argues for. A loop initialised where the passes are provably identical, whose gate the loss then barely moves, is a loop that spends its whole life near the degenerate solution. Better to start where the passes are different and find out what it costs.这放弃了带门控的循环原本最吸引人的性质——$g=1$ 时，模型在第 0 步不再复现基座 checkpoint，最初几步确实是一个从未按 96 层训练过的 96 层网络。但证据现在支持做这笔交易。一个初始化在「各遍可证明相同」之处、而损失又几乎推不动其门控的循环，等于一辈子都待在退化解旁边。不如从各遍本就不同的地方起步，看看代价是多少。

The question that run answers is the one this whole post has been circling: are the passes near-copies because the gate is small, or because the window converges to a fixed point no matter how hard you apply it? If the overlap between passes 1 and 2 drops from 0.947 once the loop runs at full strength, the gate was the constraint and the gated formulation was the mistake. If it stays at 0.947 with $g=1$, then repeated application of the same 24 layers converges regardless, and no amount of routing intervention or gate tuning will produce three paths — the loop would need to be made non-identical by construction, with per-pass parameters, and the "48 layers' worth of weights" claim goes with it.这次运行要回答的，正是全文一直在绕的那个问题：各遍之所以是近似复制品，是因为门控太小，还是因为不管你多用力施加，这个窗口都会收敛到一个不动点？如果在全强度循环下，第 1 遍与第 2 遍的重叠度从 0.947 掉下来，那约束就是门控，带门控的这套表述就是错的。如果 $g=1$ 时它仍停在 0.947，那么同样 24 层的反复施加无论如何都会收敛，任何路由干预或门控调参都产生不出三条路径——这时循环就必须在构造上被做成非同一的，为每一遍配独立参数，而「只用 48 层权重」这个卖点也就随之没了。
