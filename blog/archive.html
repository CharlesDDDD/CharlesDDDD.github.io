    Transformers Are (Naively) Looped Transformers, HorizontallyTransformer 本身就是横向展开的循环 Transformer
    The standard Transformer applies the same weights at every sequence position — making it a horizontally looped, time-recurrent architecture. The natural generalization assigns per-position weight matrices. TTT realizes this via gradient descent, and turns out to be LocoProp applied along the time axis.标准 Transformer 在每个序列位置用同一组权重——这让它成为一个横向循环、时间维递归的架构。最自然的推广是给每个位置配一组权重矩阵。TTT 用梯度下降实现了这一点，而它恰好就是沿时间轴施加的 LocoProp。

    Rethinking Key-Value Relationships in Linear Attention重新审视线性注意力中的 key-value 关系
    We tested how DeltaNet handles diverse key-value relationships from identity to exponentials, scaling up to 32,000 tokens. Under standard normalized conditions, DeltaNet stays remarkably stable whether values come from k² or e^k. Information theory explains the bounds: DeltaNet retains 99.6% of information for identity but only 1.2% for exponentials, revealing fundamental compression limits.我们测试了 DeltaNet 在各种 key-value 关系下的表现，从恒等映射到指数函数，序列最长到 32,000 token。在标准归一化条件下，无论 value 来自 k² 还是 e^k，它都稳得出奇。信息论解释了上界：恒等关系下保留 99.6% 的信息，指数关系下只剩 1.2%，这揭示了压缩能力的根本限制。
